# RotoWagon — Customer-Facing Digital Visiting Card & Equipment Showcase

Official public customer-facing digital visiting card and interactive 1:1 machinery showcase for **RotoWagon Equipment Pvt. Ltd.** (MIDC Industrial Area, Mumbai).

## Features
- **1:1 Bakery Equipment Showcase**: Rotary Rack Ovens, Rotary Deck Ovens, Spiral Mixers, Planetary Mixers, Dough Dividers.
- **Direct Lead Capture**: Quotation inquiries and specs requirements.
- **Enterprise Contact Card**: One-click vCard download (.vcf), WhatsApp messaging, and phone dialing.
- **ROI & Capacity Calculator**: Interactive calculator for commercial bakery production lines.
- **Vector QR Code**: Instantly scannable QR code generated client-side with full ECC standard.
- **Pure Public Scope**: 100% customer-facing with zero administrative portals or staff login links.

## Deployment
Deployed via Vercel.
